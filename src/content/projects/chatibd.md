ChatIBD is an AI guideline lookup assistant for clinicians who manage inflammatory bowel disease. It answers clinical questions from current IBD guidelines and cites the passages it used, with verified dosing information for adult and paediatric regimens. Clinicians can flag an answer and every answer can be traced to the sentence of the guideline corpus behind it.

I built ChatIBD and lead the project. I write about its development on the [ChatIBD blog](https://www.chatibd.com/blog).
