MARVEL is a UK multi-centre phase 2 randomised controlled trial of MitoQ, a mitochondria-targeted antioxidant, in adults with ulcerative colitis. It plans to recruit 250 patients, and its primary outcome is clinical response and remission at week 24. The study website explains who can take part and lists the participating centres and study team.
