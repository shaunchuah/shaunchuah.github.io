Foundry120 Atlas is an open index of public inflammatory bowel disease datasets, scored for how ready they are to reuse. It checks sources such as Europe PMC, NCBI GEO, ArrayExpress and CELLxGENE every day, extracts each dataset's key metadata, and scores it from 0 to 10 on cohort clarity, sample traceability, data completeness, internal consistency and accessibility. It currently covers IBD spatial transcriptomics, microbiome and single-cell datasets.
