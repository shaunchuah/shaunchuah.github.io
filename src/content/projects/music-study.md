MUSIC (with its paediatric arm, Mini-MUSIC) is a multi-centre, prospective cohort study investigating mitochondrial DAMPs as biomarkers of gut inflammation in IBD. Since 2020 it has recruited newly diagnosed adults and children across Glasgow, Edinburgh and Dundee, with a target of 250 patients, and followed them from active disease onwards. The study website has information for participants, our latest results, and audio summaries of our research made with our patient involvement group.
