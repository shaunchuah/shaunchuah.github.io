Gut Liver Glasgow is a website for gastroenterology and hepatology clinicians, trainees and researchers in the West of Scotland. Its trial finder is an early prototype: a clinician describes an anonymised patient, and an AI assistant suggests recruiting studies the patient might be eligible for. The site also lists education events such as the West of Scotland GI Teaching Day. I built it as part of a project with my GI colleagues in Glasgow.
